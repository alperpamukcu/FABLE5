4. The grade

One filter, on the game's own volume (LastCallVolume, written by LastCall → Setup Light Grade from the numbers in LightLanguage): lift toward the club's blue (0.97, 0.98, 1.04), gain toward the amber (1.04, 1.02, 0.97), contrast +12, saturation +8, a vignette of 0.22 in Night[0]. No tonemapping, no bloom (inactive), no HDR: the palette stays tokened. The HUD is a screen-space overlay and takes none of it. Post-processing is back on the room's camera for this alone; RoomGrade is the one switch.

Cost, measured in the editor's loop with vsync off, 240 frames a sample: grade off 47.93|1456.2 ms, on 48.47|1504.4 ms; off again 48.12|1492.0, on again 49.11|1504.4 (mean|worst). The difference is under the loop's own noise; the pass is one LDR full-screen blit at 1280×720.
